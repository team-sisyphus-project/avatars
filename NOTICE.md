## Removed from the fork

The upstream repository bundled **Cerebri Sans** font files under
`public/fonts/`. Cerebri Sans is a commercial typeface and was not covered by
the project's MIT licence, so those files were deleted rather than carried
forward. The app now uses the platform's own UI font.

Draftbit's logo, wordmark, favicon and preview images were also removed. Those
are Draftbit's trademarks and are not licensed for reuse.
